      Swimmers cross water and reach the plants growing in it. Everyone else has to go around. Young inherit swimming from their parent (1 in 50 differs), so a species can take to the water or give it up. This share applies to the next new world.

      Now and then a newborn gains an ability, which its offspring inherit. Weaker creatures gain camouflage or a dash; stronger ones gain spit, freeze or a pull that drags weaker enemies in. Abilities are stronger in stronger creatures and turn up more often as a species evolves.

      Living world

      Most creatures see half as far at midnight. A few are born with night eyes, which their young inherit: they see fully at night but half as far at noon.

      Now and then a creature falls sick and passes it to its own kind nearby, so crowded species suffer most. Sick creatures use more energy and have a dashed green ring; once better they cannot catch it again for a while.

      Well-fed creatures with nothing to do keep near others of their kind, which then fight off attackers together.

      A creature far out of its climate sets off north or south for a better one before stopping to feed.

      A quarter of new plants sprout beside existing ones, so plants spread out in patches and grazed-bare land is slow to grow back.
